# Security policy

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to [matrixmd@pahventure.com](mailto:matrixmd@pahventure.com) with the subject `MatrixMD Claude connector security report`.

Include the affected endpoint or file, impact, reproduction steps, and any safe supporting evidence. Do not include patient data, government identifiers, passwords, OAuth tokens, reviewer credentials, or other production secrets.

Please do not open a public issue for an unpatched vulnerability. Pah Venture S.A.S. will acknowledge the report, investigate it, and coordinate disclosure after a fix is available.

The public connector is limited to operational catalog and inventory tools. Patient, appointment, clinical, financial, administrative, and raw-SQL surfaces are out of scope because they are not exposed by this connector.
