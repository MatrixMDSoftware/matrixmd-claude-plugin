# Support

For setup, account access, permissions, or MatrixMD product help, use <https://matrixmdsoftware.com/contact> or email [matrixmd@pahventure.com](mailto:matrixmd@pahventure.com).

For connector troubleshooting, include:

- Claude surface: Claude.ai, Desktop, mobile, Cowork, or Claude Code;
- the time and timezone of the failure;
- whether the failure occurred during connection, OAuth, tool discovery, or a tool call; and
- the error message with passwords, tokens, patient data, and identifiers removed.

Use GitHub issues only for public plugin source defects that do not contain secrets or protected data.
